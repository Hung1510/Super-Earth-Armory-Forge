Super Earth Armory Forge - Armor Roles (optional pack, full edition)

Three loadouts that make light, medium and heavy armor play differently. The idea comes from
the mod "Armor Reimagined" on AyakaMods (light armor fast and fragile, heavy
armor tough and slow). This pack does not touch how damage is calculated, so it has no body
regions, no melee-only reduction and no slow-when-hit: it only uses what Armory Forge already
edits (an armor's weight class, and passive values). Nothing in it is copied from that mod.

  Light role   weight Light (fast, quick stamina), +10% move speed, +15% primary reload,
               +15% sidearm draw and reload. No extra protection.
  Medium role  weight Medium, +25 armor, 10% less chest and explosive damage.
  Heavy role   weight Heavy (slow), +75 armor, 25% less chest and explosive damage,
               30% less impact damage and stagger, 15% slower primary reload.

The role applies to whatever armor you wear (the "Every armor" stack), so a heavy-looking armor
can be set to Light. The values are in the .ini files: open one in Notepad and change a number.

Use it
  1. Install Super Earth Armory Forge (the full edition, 7.3 or newer) and start the game.
  2. Open one of the .ini files in Notepad, press Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C.
  3. In game press F7, open the Armor tab, click "Paste code". The role is now your stack
     (Undo brings back what you had). Open the Presets tab and "+ Save current stack" to keep it
     and swap between the three with F9.
  Or in the web builder (https://hung1510.github.io/Super-Earth-Armory-Forge/): Import, pick a file.
